About

I have always been fascinated by neurons, their complexity and the way they connect to form intricate networks.

Over the years, I have enjoyed producing, analyzing, and ultimately publishing data on synapses — the structures that enable communication between neurons. In 2023, I moved into industry to continue pursuing my passion: research involving structured and unstructured data.

My work combines deep neuroscience expertise with modern machine learning, allowing me to bridge the worlds of academic research and applied AI.
