About me

I always loved neurons, their complexity and the way they connect with each other creating networks. I enjoyed many years producing, analyzing and, finally, publishing data about synapses (which are the structures that facilitate communication between neurons). Now I’ve decided to move to the industry with the hope of finding an opportunity to continue working with my passion: research with structured and/or unstructured data.
Work-style:
Able to learn new knowledge and adapt quickly to new environments. Strong independent work style and excellent teamwork skills. Well-organized and passionate.
