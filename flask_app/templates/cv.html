About

I have always been fascinated by neurons, their complexity and the way they connect to form intricate networks. Over the years, I have enjoyed producing, analyzing, and ultimately publishing data on synapses — the structures that enable communication between neurons. In 2023, I decided to move into industry to continue pursuing my passion: research involving structured and/or unstructured data.

Work-style: Able to learn new knowledge and adapt quickly to new environments. Strong independent work style and excellent teamwork skills. Well-organized and passionate.
