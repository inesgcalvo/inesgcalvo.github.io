Neuropapers DB & Journal Predictor

A complete ETL pipeline scraping 61,945 neuroscience publications from PubMed (2010–2023), enriched with geographical and institutional data, loaded into a MySQL relational database. Topped with an ML classification model using TensorFlow embeddings + Quadratic Discriminant Analysis to predict the most suitable journal for a new paper given its title and abstract.
